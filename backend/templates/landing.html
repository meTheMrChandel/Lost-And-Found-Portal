How Asset Recovery Works

01

1. Submit a Report

Report lost or found items with location tags, photo attachments, and private hidden proof only verified by campus moderators.

02

2. Automated Match

Our algorithmic engine continuously calculates multi-criteria similarity scores across category, time window, and location keywords.

03

3. Safe Handover

Claim items securely via single-use cryptographically generated handover codes in the security office or through direct meeting.
